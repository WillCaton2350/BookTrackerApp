Personal Favorites:

Absolute Batman

Written by Scott Snyder, this new take on Batman from his origins to the relationships with his friends from crime alley.

Absolute Batman - Vol.2

Written by Scott Snyder, with the introduction of Bane and Killer Croc, this story perfectly expands the new world of Gotham city and the vision of artist Nick Dragotta.

Barda

Written by Ngozi Ukazu, this story reveals a softer side of Barda where she meets Scott Free. We get an insight into her origins, life on Apokolips and her relationship with the furies.

The New Gods - Vol.1

Written by Ram V with artwork by Evan Cagle, we get into the relationship of Barda and Scott Free caring for their daughter as Orion asks Scott to help him defy the wishes of Highfather and save a new God born in India.

The New Gods - Vol.2

Written by Ram V with artwork by Evan Cagle, this take on the New Gods connects to the Absolute Universe and the death of Darkseid in an epic journey through the fourth world to protect Kamaal. The newest addition. A New God.

Batman 2025

Written By Matt Fraction, we explore Gotham City with new villains like the Ojo and the Minotaur. We are introduced to new and old characters like Anika Zeller, Hugo Strange the Penguin and more.

Adam: Legend of the Blue Marvel

Written by Kevin Grevioux, This story takes us through the Jim Crow era the many problems america would have with a black superhero. He must team up with the Avengers to defeat Anti-Man.

Rook: Exodus

Written by Geoff Johns and Jason Fabok, we are introduced to a dying planet that is surviving on a world engine. All of the animals are electronic and are used in a civil war between our main characters.

Batwing

Written by Judd Winnick, an African based superhero named David Zavimbe is scouted by Bruce Wayne and arms him with the ability to protect the people and intrest of Africa.

King In Black

Written by Donny Cates with artwork by Stegman, this is the conclusion of the Venom series where Eddie Brock with help from the Avengers defeat Knull in a battle to protect the Earth.

The Ultimates

Written by Deniz Camp, this is a new take on the villains from the marvel universe stemming from the King in Black run where The Maker, an evil Reed Richards from another dimension reigns supreme.

Dawn Runner

Written by Ram V with artwork by Evan Cagle, this story is about a cyberpunk themed world where people in massive machines battle for the fate of their world.
